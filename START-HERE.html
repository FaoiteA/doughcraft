Open DoughCraft

Install it like a real app

Install DoughCraft to your home screen so it opens like any other app — no browser bar, no clutter.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Open index.html in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home Screen)
  4. Confirm — DoughCraft appears on your home screen

On iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open index.html in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow at the bottom)
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add — DoughCraft appears as an app icon

On desktop (Windows / Mac / Linux)

  1. Double-click index.html — it opens in your default browser
  2. Bookmark it or pin the tab for quick access
  3. Chrome and Edge also let you install it: click the install icon in the address bar

Your first bake

  1. Tap a recipe — try Same-Day Pan Pizza for a quick weeknight bake, or Focaccia for a beginner-friendly loaf
  2. Read the ingredients and method — for pizza, use the calculator to scale for the number of balls and size you want
  3. Tap Start Recipe, pick when you want to eat, and the app builds your entire timeline backwards from that moment
  4. Follow along step by step — the ring counts down each step and tells you exactly what to do
  5. When you're done, log the bake with a rating and notes so you can compare next time
About long ferments

Bulk fermentations, cold retards, and overnight ferments can run 8+ hours. Your phone will sleep during that time — the app can't wake it up. For any step longer than 2 hours, DoughCraft will show a card asking you to set a phone alarm as a backup. Do it. It takes 15 seconds and saves your dough.

Your data stays yours

Everything you log — bakes, ratings, notes, favorites — is saved on your device only. There's no account, no server, no cloud sync. That means it's private, but it also means you're responsible for backing it up.

To back up your journal

Open the app → Profile tab → Export journal. This downloads a JSON file with everything. Save it to Google Drive, iCloud, email it to yourself — whatever you trust.

To restore

Profile → Import journal → pick your JSON file. All your data comes back.

Tip

Back up once a month. It takes 10 seconds and it's the only way to move your journal to a new phone or recover if you clear your browser data.

Common questions

Do I need internet to use DoughCraft?

No. Once you've opened it once, it runs entirely offline. Recipes, timers, calculators — everything works without a connection.

Will DoughCraft wake my phone during an overnight ferment?

No. Web apps can't wake phones — only native apps installed from the Play Store or App Store can do that. For long ferments, use the "Copy time" button on the alarm card and set a native phone alarm as backup.

Can I use DoughCraft on multiple devices?

Yes. Copy the folder to any device and open index.html. Each device has its own separate journal — use Export / Import to sync bakes between them.

Will I get updates?

Yes. When new features or recipes ship, you'll get an email from Gumroad. Download the new ZIP and replace your files — your bake journal stays intact if you export/import it.

The calculator amounts look odd for tiny quantities (yeast, salt)

Small amounts round to one decimal (e.g. 0.4g yeast). For quantities under 5g, use a precision scale if you have one — a normal kitchen scale usually reads in whole grams. If your scale doesn't show decimals, round up — a tiny bit extra yeast or salt won't ruin a bake.

Can I add my own recipes?

Not in this version — DoughCraft ships with a curated library of tested recipes. Custom recipes are on the roadmap for future updates.
